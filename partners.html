Why partner with us

You don't build a product from zero. You don't carry the engineering risk. You bring the relationships and market knowledge, we bring three tools people already pay for.

Building software from scratch to enter a new region is slow and expensive — the product has to be built, tested, and proven before you can sell a single seat. ExportReady, 1099watch.com, and InvoraPro have already cleared that bar. What's missing in most new markets isn't a better product; it's someone on the ground who understands local businesses well enough to get them to say yes.

That's the gap a Cebean partner fills — and it's why the model is profit-sharing rather than a one-time referral fee. Your income grows with the accounts you bring in and keep, not just the ones you close once.

Two ways to partner

Pick the level of involvement that matches how you want to work.

REFERRAL PARTNER

Send us the lead, we close it

Best fit: consultants, associations, and advisors with client relationships but no interest in selling software directly.

  • Introduce Cebean to businesses that fit one of the three products
  • We run the sales conversation and close the account
  • You earn a revenue share for the life of the account
  • Minimal time commitment beyond the introduction
REGIONAL PARTNER

Sell and support it in your market

Best fit: operators, agencies, or firms who want ownership of a territory or vertical and are set up to sell and support clients directly.

  • Sell, onboard, and provide first-line support in your region or industry
  • Co-branded materials and market-specific positioning support
  • A larger revenue share reflecting the greater involvement
  • Priority input on features that matter for your market
