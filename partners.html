Why partner with us

You don't build a product from zero. You don't carry the engineering risk. You bring the relationships and market knowledge — we bring three working products, each already built, tested, and live in market.

Building software from scratch to enter a new region is slow and expensive — the product has to be built, tested, and proven before you can sell a single seat. ExportReady, 1099watch.com, and InvoraPro have already cleared that bar: real, live products you can demo on day one, not a concept on a slide. What's missing in most new markets isn't a better product; it's someone on the ground who understands local businesses well enough to get them to say yes.

That's the gap a Cebean partner fills — and it's why the model is profit-sharing, not a base salary or a flat one-time referral fee. Your income grows with the accounts you bring in and keep, not just the ones you close once.
