Meet Pascale

Twenty years of enterprise lessons, so you don't have to learn them the hard way.

I've sat inside dozens of implementation projects: the highs, the lows, the risks nobody flagged until it was too late. I started Cebean to put that experience to work for businesses that don't have twenty years to spare learning it themselves.

Why I do both

I founded Cebean in 2004. Consulting came first. The same gaps kept repeating: manual work, untracked risk, growth plans with no readiness behind them, so I started building software to close them for good. ExportReady and 1099watch both started exactly that way. For hands-on builds, I bring in a trusted network of developers, architects, and specialists, scoped to what the project actually needs, not a fixed bench sitting on a bill. I also work bilingually (EN/FR), natively, not just translated.

Twenty years, every kind of industry

Higher education, retail, telecom, transportation, banking, insurance, construction, public sector. Different industries. Same patterns underneath.

●Same challenges

●Same fear from leadership: will this work, will my team accept it

Every lesson carries forward to the next client. That's why clients notice the difference - and why stakeholders trust it gets done right.

Why this work, still

I genuinely like this: the people, the collaboration, solving a real problem together. Not a tagline. The part that keeps me doing it.

What you actually get

Five commitments that shape how it feels to work with me, not just what I deliver.

01

A fix for your problem, not a generic playbook

Every recommendation and every product feature ties to a specific risk or gap I found in your situation - never a best-practice template stretched to fit.

02

You'll always know where things stand

Clear, direct updates - in English or French - so you're never left guessing whether something's on track or quietly behind.

03

A pace that matches your reality

Not rushed to hit a rollout deadline, not stalled waiting on us either. Especially with AI: readiness and change management come before the tool, not after.

04

Outcomes, not paperwork

Success is measured by whether the fix actually worked - not by whether a deck got produced or a milestone got checked off on time.

05

I stay invested until it's actually working

Present through delivery and into adoption. Your success is how I measure mine - not the signed contract.
