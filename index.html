Why both

You don't get a slide deck and a handshake. You get either a plan built to run, or a tool that runs itself — whichever actually solves it.

Cebean started as a consulting practice, and consulting is still the front door for most engagements. But after two decades in IT and business analysis across public and private sector work, the same gaps kept appearing: manual processes nobody had time to automate, compliance risk nobody was tracking, growth plans with no readiness behind them.

Rather than write another recommendation for someone else to build, we started building — first for our own clients, now as standalone products anyone can use. Every tool we ship starts as a pattern we saw in the field, not a feature we thought sounded good.
