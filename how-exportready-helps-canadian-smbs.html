Unlocking International Markets: How ExportReady Can Help Canadian SMBs Expand Beyond the U.S.

Written by Pascale Décosse · Jan 5, 2026

As a Canadian SMB, you may be feeling the pressure of relying solely on the U.S. market. Expanding beyond it can unlock new growth potential, but knowing how to get started — or whether your business is truly ready — can be overwhelming. That's where ExportReady comes in.

What is ExportReady?

ExportReady is an easy-to-use online assessment that guides businesses through questions evaluating nine key pillars of export readiness — strategy and market selection, product/service readiness, cultural competence, financial preparedness, internal operations, digital commerce capabilities, risk management, staff knowledge, and cultural awareness. See the full pillar breakdown for how each one works.

After completing the assessment, you receive a comprehensive report showing where your business stands across all nine pillars, along with actionable recommendations for your next steps.

How ExportReady benefits Canadian SMBs

Clarify your starting point — an overall export readiness score helps you understand strengths and areas for improvement, so you prioritize the right actions instead of wasting time on unimportant ones.

Visualize your score across key pillars — see exactly where your strengths lie and where the gaps are, with a complete, holistic view rather than a single number.

Receive actionable recommendations — tailored advice on next steps, potential challenges, and the resources you'll need.

Simplify your export planning — a clear action plan instead of an overwhelming blank page.

Save time with curated resources — a centralized calendar of relevant trade events and curated resources, instead of hours spent searching.

Why Canadian SMBs should use it

The global marketplace offers tremendous growth opportunities, but expanding without the right tools and insight can lead to costly mistakes. ExportReady removes the guesswork and gives you a structured, pillar-based approach — whether you're looking at Europe, Asia, Latin America, or simply reducing reliance on the U.S.

If you're ready to start, the first step is easy — take the ExportReady assessment and see exactly where your business stands.
