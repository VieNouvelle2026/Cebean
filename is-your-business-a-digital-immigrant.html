DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION

Is Your Business a Digital Immigrant?

Written by Pascale Décosse · May 1, 2022

Although the term "immigration" sounds a bit strange when applied to a business, it highlights a useful contrast: Digitally Native companies (Uber, Airbnb, Shopify) versus the many, many businesses that are not.

Any business whose core operation was founded with "bricks and mortar" — relying on physical assets, physical products, or face-to-face sales transactions — could be described as a Digital Immigrant. Why?

1. Your products and services are not digital in nature; you need to transform them or create new ones. 2. You rely on a physical space and fixed location to sell; you haven't yet leveraged cloud capabilities to "rent" your operating infrastructure. 3. You don't use artificial intelligence as part of your operating model — to either automate tasks or gather data for more informed decisions.

Digital transformation is what helps Digital Immigrant businesses evolve closer to the Digitally Native business model.
