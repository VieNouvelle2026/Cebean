ExportReady

exportready.cebean.com

What you get: a clear answer to "are we actually ready to expand," in minutes instead of months — and a prioritized to-do list instead of a vague sense of risk.

ExportReady exists because a very specific, urgent problem kept showing up: Canadian SMBs who had relied on the U.S. market for years, never needed a plan B, and suddenly did. Roughly three-quarters of Canadian exports go to a single market — if that's you, ExportReady turns the same diagnostic our trade consulting practice runs by hand into a self-serve assessment, so you can move on a new market with actual confidence instead of a guess.

  • 18 free questions, or a full 45-question assessment for a detailed breakdown
  • Risk alerts and a clear export-readiness classification
  • A prioritized roadmap with timelines and effort levels
  • Direct links to EDC, FITT, BDC, and the Trade Commissioner Service

Contractor Audit Shield

1099watch.com

What you get: the answer to "how exposed are we" before an auditor asks the question — and a paper trail that backs you up if they do.

Contractor Audit Shield was built as automated compliance management — replacing what's still a manual, easy-to-neglect process at most businesses. Misclassifying a contractor is easy to do and expensive to discover late — back pay, penalties, and legal fees add up fast, especially for U.S. businesses that hire a lot of contract labour. The tool exists to mitigate that legal and lawsuit risk before it materializes: organizing exactly who you're working with, how, and where your exposure sits, so you find out on your terms.

  • Risk flags and compliance logs across your contractor base
  • Supply chain structure and end-client visibility
  • Bulk upload and activity tracking for larger teams
  • A UK/IR35-aligned version for cross-border operations
