ExportReady

exportready.cebean.com

What you get: a clear answer to "are we actually ready to expand," in minutes instead of months — and a prioritized to-do list instead of a vague sense of risk.

Roughly three-quarters of Canadian exports go to a single market. If that's you, ExportReady turns the same diagnostic our trade consulting practice runs by hand into a self-serve assessment — so you can see your exposure and your next move before you spend a dollar on expansion.

  • 18 free questions, or a full 45-question assessment for a detailed breakdown
  • Risk alerts and a clear export-readiness classification
  • A prioritized roadmap with timelines and effort levels
  • Direct links to EDC, FITT, BDC, and the Trade Commissioner Service

Contractor Audit Shield

1099watch.com

What you get: the answer to "how exposed are we" before an auditor asks the question — and a paper trail that backs you up if they do.

Misclassifying a contractor is easy to do and expensive to discover late — back pay, penalties, and legal fees add up fast. Contractor Audit Shield organizes the data that actually protects you: who you're working with, how, and exactly where your risk sits, so you find out on your terms.

  • Risk flags and compliance logs across your contractor base
  • Supply chain structure and end-client visibility
  • Bulk upload and activity tracking for larger teams
  • A UK/IR35-aligned version for cross-border operations
Visit 1099watch.com →

InvoraPro

invorapro.com

What you get: invoices that go out before you've left the driveway, a system that chases late payers so you don't have to make the awkward call, and a way to spot a bad contract before you've signed it.

Independent contractors and trades get squeezed from both sides — late or missing payments on one end, scope creep and risky contract terms on the other. InvoraPro runs the whole job from quote to paid: client sign-off happens on your phone at the job site, invoices build themselves from logged work and expenses, and overdue accounts get chased automatically. The Pro tier adds AI contract review and client risk monitoring, so you know who you're dealing with before the work starts, not after the invoice goes unpaid.

  • Quote, on-site client sign-off, and one-tap invoicing
  • Automated collections for overdue invoices
  • AI contract review with plain-English risk flags (Pro)
  • Client risk monitoring and payment-behaviour tracking (Pro)
  • Live in the UK, Canada, USA, Australia, and New Zealand
