Learn Through Experimentation — You Might Fail

Written by Pascale Décosse · Jun 1, 2022

Encourage more experimentation with new products, apps, workflows, and software before committing to a big change. "Try before you buy" is sound advice — experiment before you fully commit.

As part of your digital transformation, consider putting together small teams who can come up with an idea, formulate a hypothesis, conduct an experiment, and collect results. It sounds scientific, but it's a great way to learn quickly through experimentation before committing at a bigger scale. If the experiment fails, that's okay — it's a learning opportunity too.

This approach can improve your core capabilities and even give you a strategic advantage, if you empower your internal resources to become proficient at it. You'll end up with an experienced, trusted, self-managed team conducting small but valuable experiments and sharing results to inform your go/no-go decisions. See how this connects to embedding innovation in your culture more broadly.
